Data we process

We process your account identifier, action definitions and source, variables, encrypted secrets, schedules, request/response excerpts, trigger inputs, run outcomes, IP-based rate-limit counters, ledger and audit records. Secret reads return metadata only. Store credentials as secrets rather than plaintext variables or source. Trigger URLs and keys are sensitive credentials.

Providers and destinations

Cloudflare hosts compute, encrypted storage, queues and scheduling. Clerk provides sign-in. Stripe handles card details and deposits; cronly receives payment identifiers, amounts and status rather than full card numbers. Anthropic processes text and bounded account context only when you invoke metered prose features. Customer secret values are not decrypted for prose. Your actions send the data you configure to the destinations you choose.

Retention and access

Run records retain at most 30 days or the latest 1,000 per job. Ledger and change audit records are retained for the account's lifetime and may require statutory retention after closure. You can export definitions and retained runs through Account and the API. Secret values are write-only and cannot be exported. Contact [PRIVACY EMAIL] to request account deletion or exercise applicable rights.

Security and choices

Secrets use account-derived AES-GCM encryption. Logs redact values used in execution and encoded forms; excerpts are displayed as text. Browser local mode stores only a development identity in sessionStorage. Production sign-in uses Clerk's cookies/session storage. We do not use advertising trackers.
