Deposits

Minimum $5 in credit. The card payment cost is itemized on top. For credit C cents, total payment is ceil((C + 30) / 0.971) cents. Your balance receives the full credit amount. A $5 credit therefore costs $5.46. New accounts may deposit at most $20 per payment during their first week.

Unused credit does not expire while the account exists. Refunds of unused credit are available on request, minus non-refundable card payment costs. See the draft Terms for the policy under review.

Limits protect the balance

Jobs can have daily/monthly budgets; keys can have daily caps. Refusals use HTTP 402 and pause schedules rather than repeatedly logging unfunded runs. Retries are separate paid runs. An uncertain lost result settles at its hold. Projections are maximum-cost forecasts, not a guarantee of an execution date.

How we set prices

We ignore included provider allowances, round costs up, and require prices after an 8% revenue haircut to exceed 1.5 times the estimated worst-case marginal cost. We include reservation, settlement, queue, gateway and retention writes. Provider rates and the model are tested in the repository; operational measurements will recalibrate these estimates after launch.
